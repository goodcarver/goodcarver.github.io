Program description

Good Carver operates an SMS messaging program to send subscribers updates about upcoming shows, new music releases, and similar announcements. Messages are sent from a dedicated phone number belonging to Good Carver.

How to opt in

You can join the list in two ways:

  • Keyword: Text JOIN to our number. You will receive a confirmation reply.
  • Web form: Submit the sign-up form on www.goodcarver.com with your mobile number and consent checkbox checked.

By opting in, you consent to receive recurring automated marketing text messages from Good Carver at the number you provide. Consent is not a condition of any purchase.

Message frequency

We send approximately 1 message per month. Message frequency may vary.

Message and data rates

Message and data rates may apply. Contact your wireless carrier for details about your plan.

How to opt out

Reply STOP to any message at any time to unsubscribe. You will receive a one-time confirmation message and no further messages will be sent. Supported opt-out keywords: STOP, END, CANCEL, UNSUBSCRIBE, QUIT.

How to get help

Reply HELP for help. You can also contact us at goodcarvermusic@gmail.com.
