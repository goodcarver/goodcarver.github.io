Messaging Privacy Policy

Good Carver — Last updated July 2026

What we collect

When you opt in to receive text messages from Good Carver — either by texting a keyword to our number or by submitting the sign-up form on this website — we collect your mobile phone number and the date and method of your opt-in. We do not collect your name, email address, or any other personal information through the messaging program.

How we use your information

Your phone number is used solely to send you SMS messages about Good Carver: upcoming shows, new music releases, and similar updates. We send approximately one message per month. We do not use your number for any other purpose.

Sharing your information

We do not sell, rent, or share your phone number or any other personal information collected through this program with third parties for their marketing or promotional purposes. Your number may be passed to our SMS service provider (Twilio) solely for the purpose of delivering messages to you.

Opt-out / data deletion

You may stop receiving messages at any time by replying STOP to any message. After opting out, your number will be marked inactive and no further messages will be sent. To request deletion of your phone number from our records, reply STOP or contact us at goodcarvermusic@gmail.com.

Data retention

We retain your phone number for as long as you remain opted in to receive messages. If you opt out, your record is retained in an inactive state to honor your opt-out preference and prevent accidental re-enrollment. You may request full deletion at any time by emailing us.

Security

Subscriber data is stored in a private database. We take reasonable technical measures to protect it from unauthorized access. No internet transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email goodcarvermusic@gmail.com.
